Emotional Resonance
Tags: #Reflections close #EveningThoughts close #WalksInRain close

There is a particular stillness that settles over the city around eight in the evening, when the rush of commutes fades into the quiet murmur of dinner tables and warm lamplight. The frantic cadence that commands the morning slowly dismantles itself, letting the room breathe once again.

I walked through St. James Park on my way home tonight. The mist off the pond carried the sharp, earthy fragrance of damp fallen leaves. For the first time in weeks, my mind felt unburdened by upcoming deadlines, detached from the subtle hum of perpetual urgency that usually trails me like an uninvited shadow.

“

“In the midst of winter, I found there was, within me, an invincible summer.”

— Albert Camus

I want to remember this sensation: the feeling that nothing needs immediate fixing, that existing in this hour is entirely sufficient. The ink on this page dries slowly, just like the evening. I watched an amber moth flutter against the library lamp and realized how rare it has become to simply witness time rather than frantically account for it.

• • •

Tomorrow will bring its own demands, yet tonight remains unblemished. A quiet victory over noise.
